The Agra Shoe Market

A 500-Year Legacy of Leather Craftsmanship

From the Mughal Era to Modern Exports

The story of Agra's leather footwear industry dates back to the 16th century during the Mughal era. Initially producing fine leather goods, saddles, and armor for the royal court, the local artisans passed down their intricate skills generation after generation. Today, Agra is globally recognized as the "Shoe Capital of India."

The Footwear Capital of India

Agra is the undisputed powerhouse of Indian footwear manufacturing. It accounts for approximately 65% of India's domestic footwear demand and contributes a staggering 28% of the country's total footwear exports. Hundreds of thousands of highly skilled artisans work in the city, ensuring that the legacy of hand-crafted leather lives on alongside modern, mechanized manufacturing.

The Prestigious GI Tag (2023)

In August 2023, the Government of India officially awarded the Geographical Indication (GI) tag to Agra's leather footwear. This prestigious status acts as a global seal of authenticity, confirming that shoes manufactured in Agra possess unique qualities and reputation tied directly to this region's heritage. When you source from Agra, you are buying a product backed by a government-certified legacy.
