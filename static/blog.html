The Legacy of Agra's Shoemaking

How the Mughal era shaped the craftsmanship of India's biggest footwear hub.

Agra has been the heart of India's shoemaking industry since the Mughal era. Artisans, known as 'Karigars', have passed down their meticulous leather-working skills through generations. Today, Agra produces over 65% of domestic footwear and is recognized globally for its export-grade quality.


By sourcing directly from Agra factories like Karakorum, B2B buyers eliminate middlemen and ensure they receive authentic, high-quality products. With the recent 2023 GI (Geographical Indication) Tag, Agra footwear guarantees authenticity and export-grade craftsmanship. It is simply the most profitable decision for your retail or wholesale business.
