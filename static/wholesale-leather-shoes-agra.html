Yes. We regularly supply white-label footwear to boutique retail chains across Mumbai, Bangalore, and Chandigarh with custom logo stamping and brand boxes.

Sample orders can be verified upfront. Bulk dispatches work via standard B2B terms with bill of lading / transport receipts shared immediately upon dispatch.
